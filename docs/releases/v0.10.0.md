Table Pro Max 0.10.0 makes search forgiving, shows SQL Server query plans, and makes MongoDB deletes and large restores safer and faster.

If you have 0.1.1 or later, the app offers this update itself: a banner appears under the title bar, or go to **Settings › Updates › Check now**.

## New
### Search
- **Search forgives the way you type**, in the Ctrl+K palette, the sidebar's **Filter objects** and the connections search on the home screen. Words can come in any order and match anywhere in a name, its schema or its connection; letters in order and one typo in a word of four letters or more also match. `order items`, `oi` and `odrer_items` all find `public.order_items`. The matched letters are highlighted and the closest matches come first.

### SQL Server
- **Query plans.** **Explain** shows the estimated plan, and **Analyze** runs the statement and shows the actual one (after the usual confirmation for statements that change data). Both are drawn as a tree of operators with the table and index each one reads, its predicate, its cost, and estimated against actual rows.
- **Named instances.** Enter an instance name (`SQLEXPRESS`) in the new **Instance** field, or `host\instance` in an address. Without a port, the app asks the server's SQL Browser service (UDP 1434) for it.
- **`GO n`** runs the batch before it n times, as in SSMS and sqlcmd (up to 1,000).

### Restore points
- **Large restores are much faster.** Rows that come back are inserted 1,000 at a time (fewer where the database limits parameters) instead of one statement per row; a 60,000-row restore takes about two seconds on a local server. The review shows the statements that will run.

### MongoDB
- **Deleting by filter removes exactly the documents that were counted.** The app reads their `_id`s, checks there are as many as you confirmed, and deletes those, in batches of 10,000 (at most 1,000,000 at once). A document that starts matching after the count is no longer deleted with them.
- A delete that times out says how many documents were already deleted and how many more may have been.
- The connection check is a plain `ping`, and expanding a database reads collection counts for at most 3 seconds; collections not reached show without a count.
- Pages stop before a document that would take them past 16 MB, and find and aggregate wait no longer than the timeout.
- Integers above 2^53 show exactly in the table view, not rounded.
- One operation runs at a time per connection, as for the other databases.

### Fixed
- The Ctrl+K palette: pressing Enter on a connection now connects. Before, it could cancel the connection it had just started.

## Please read before installing
- Search: matches with a typo are listed after every match without one.
- SQL Server: named instances through an SSH tunnel need the instance's port, since SQL Browser is reached over UDP.
- MongoDB: Atlas itself is still untested (only the Atlas local image).
- Installers are not code-signed. On Windows, choose *More info* and then *Run anyway*. On macOS, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Table Pro Max.app"`. In-app updates are signed separately and verified before they install.
